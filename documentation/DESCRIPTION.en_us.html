Mckitrick is a condensed display typeface drawn from a single sheet of hand-cut marker sketches. Every letter is built from separate, flat-ended strokes that stop short of one another where they meet, the way the originals were drawn. The dots on i and j are tapered, wider at the top, and the same dot carries through the punctuation and accents.

It works best at display sizes: headings, posters, signs, labels and short lines of text. A stylistic set (ss01) swaps in a long-tailed f, taken from the margin of the original sketch.

To contribute, see github.com/ianlawton/McKitrick.
