FONTLOG for Mckitrick
=====================

This file provides detailed information on the Mckitrick font software. It is
distributed with the fonts under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.


Basic Font Information
----------------------

Mckitrick is a condensed display typeface drawn from a sheet of hand-cut
marker sketches. Each letter is built from separate flat-ended strokes; the
dots on i and j taper, wider at the top. One weight (Regular), 349 glyphs,
covering the Google Fonts Latin Core character set. OpenType features: kern,
mark, ccmp (soft-dotted i and j), case (capital-height dashes and
guillemets), and ss01/salt for an alternative f with a long descender.

The glyphs are defined as centre-line strokes in Python (sources/glyphs.py)
and expanded to outlines, which are fitted with smooth cubic curves
(sources/fitcurves.py). Sidebearings and kerning are measured from the
outlines (sources/spacing.py). sources/build.py writes the UFO source, and
the fonts are compiled from it with gftools builder (sources/config.yaml).
The original pencil sketch is included in sources/sketch/.

The design and the original sketches are by Ian Lawton. The build tooling,
the extrapolated glyphs and the spacing were developed with the help of
Claude (Anthropic), an AI assistant, working from those sketches under Ian
Lawton's direction and review.


ChangeLog
---------

19 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) Mckitrick v2.006
- Straight edges are now single straight lines: the bars of E F L T Z Æ Œ
  sit flush with the cap height and baseline, the bowls of B D P R b leave
  the stem square, and the a/d/æ bowls no longer dip below the stem foot.
  Diagonal feet (K R k) and the N diagonal are cut cleanly; redundant points
  on straight lines are removed and small corner curves stay curves
- Ħ crossbar extends clearly beyond the stems
- Added Ţ ţ (T and t with cedilla)
- Kerning keeps the f and the descending f (ss01) clear of quotes and of
  accents on the following letter (ï ì î ğ č š ř and others)
- Superiors, ordinals and fractions redrawn lighter (stems about 70% of the
  main stem) on a shared baseline; fraction parts no longer touch the slash
- Figures 2 3 5 6 9: round tops and bottoms overshoot consistently with 0
- ģ: the turned-comma accent floats clear of the bowl
- New case feature: hyphen, dashes, guillemets, middle dot and bullet centred
  on the cap height for all-caps text
- The OTF is now hinted (otfautohint) and no longer carries a TrueType prep
  table

18 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) Mckitrick v2.005
- m, n and h shoulders now keep an even stroke width all the way over: the
  underside runs parallel to the top, with only a small radius where it meets
  the stems. The shoulder is a little lighter than the stems and branches
  slightly higher, so the tops of these letters carry less ink in text

18 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) Mckitrick v2.004
- Lightened the m, n and h shoulders: they taper where they leave the stem
  and ease off over the crown, reaching full weight only as they become the
  next stem
- The undersides of the shoulders now curve gently, and the corners of the
  counters (at the joint and under the crown) are rounded instead of sharp

18 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) Mckitrick v2.003
- Smoothed the tops of the m, n and h shoulders: the steep diagonal now
  curves over into the stem instead of meeting a flat, squared-off top

18 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) Mckitrick v2.002
- Redrew the shoulders of m, n and h to follow the original sketch: each
  shoulder branches from the stem about halfway up, rises as a steep,
  nearly straight diagonal and turns sharply into a flat-topped stem, so
  the counters come to a point under the shoulder

14 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) Mckitrick v2.001
- Fixed bowed stems and diagonals left by the curve fitting in v2.000:
  N, M, Y, h, m, n, u, y, µ and ¥ (and their accented forms). Long straight
  edges are now always kept straight, so every outline stays within about
  2 units of the drawn shape

9 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) Mckitrick v2.000
- Family renamed from McKitrick to Mckitrick, following the Google Fonts
  naming rules
- Google Fonts Latin Core coverage: 118 new glyphs for Central and Eastern
  European languages (macron, breve, ogonek, dot above, double acute and
  comma-below accents; Đ, Ħ, Ł, capital sharp s, dotless j), combining
  accents, the minus sign and the dotted circle
- Outlines redrawn as smooth cubic curves with points on extremes
- Mark positioning for combining accents; soft-dotted i and j (ccmp)
- Kerning reorganised into classes, with exceptions so accents don't collide
- Maths signs share one width
- Built with gftools builder; hinted TTF, OTF and WOFF2

9 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) McKitrick v1.500
- Released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Completed ASCII, Latin-1 and Windows-1252: 83 new glyphs, including
  tilde, ring and caron accents, Æ, Ø, ß, Ð, Þ, currency and other symbols,
  superiors and fractions
- Vertical metrics, gasp and other font tables set to current
  recommendations
- Added the editable UFO source

8 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) McKitrick v1.400
- Added the middle dot (·)
- Overshoot for round letters (o, e, O, 0, 8)
- Reworked &, S, s and £

7 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) McKitrick v1.100
- Optical spacing and generated kerning

7 October 2026 (Ian Lawton) McKitrick v1.000
- First version, drawn from the original sketch of i t r i j c k l g m f
